## Changes made from this cross-check
See the "2026-11-11 cross-check" comments in model.js. In short: C Street and Ventura Harbor ideal size raised; Ventura Harbor, HB North/South and Uppers get S + W combo credit; County Line and Zuma best direction moved from NW to S–W; Leo Carrillo and Newport Lower Jetties direction windows widened toward SSE; tide windows lowered for the Ventura spots, Leo Carrillo and the Wedge; Wedge and Lower Jetties ideal size raised.
