## 54th St, Newport (Surfline: "Newport Upper Jetties")

Faces ~220° (SSW). Offshore wind = NE/N/E. Jetty-assisted beach break: fast lefts on the north side of the groins on S swells, better rights on W swells. Newport submarine canyon focuses energy toward 56th.

| Tier | Swell direction | Period | Face height | Tide | Wind |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Perfect** | S/SSW 175–205°, or W 260–280° + S combo | 13–18 s | 4–6 ft | 2–4 ft, rising | NE/N/E ≤10 kt, or glassy |
| **Good** | 165–215° or 255–285° | 11–20 s | 3–7 ft | 1–4.5 ft | Offshore, or onshore ≤6 kt |
| **Mid / Fun** | 160–290° | 9–20 s | 2–3 or 7–8 ft | 0–5.5 ft | Onshore 4–5 kt (≤6 mph) |
| **Don't Go / Poor** | >295° (NW blocked) or <155° | <8 s | <1.5 or >8–9 ft (closeouts) | <0 or >5.5 ft | **Onshore over 6 mph (5.2 kt)** — your call, 2026-11-11 |

- Catalina sits ~222–256° from here *(est. from map bearings)*: short-period swell from that arc loses ~40%; long period wraps.
- Very long-period S (17–20 s, ~3.5 ft deep water) closes out much of Newport.
- Minus tides: sand-sucking, closey. 5 ft+ highs: fat, backwash. Rising tide generally better.
- Typical day: glassy/light AM, WNW–WSW sea breeze 10–18 kt in the afternoon. Jetties hold up in light onshore better than open beach.
- Sources disagree on the best direction (S vs SSW vs SW).

Sources: surfnerd.com/surf-forecast/newport-upper-jetties · socalforecast.blogspot.com (56th St and Lower Jetties break maps, 2008; "SoCal's North Pacific window") · surf-forecast.com/breaks/Fifty-sixth-St · thesurfatlas.com · windfinder (Balboa Pier) · Surfline spot guide: "SSE, S, SW; also short period W, WNW; combo… E, NE wind… chest–overhead… medium tide".

## Malibu: 1st / 2nd / 3rd Point

Faces ~S (165–195°). Right-hand cobblestone point. Offshore = N/NE; NW is side-offshore and the point is partly sheltered from it.

| Tier | Swell direction | Period | Face height | Tide | Wind | Sections |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Perfect** | 185–225° | ≥15 s | 4–8 ft | 1.5–4 ft, falling | Glassy or N–NE ≤8 kt | 3rd → 2nd → 1st connect |
| **Good** | 175–240° | 13–18 s | 3–5 ft | 1–4.5 ft | N–NE ≤12 kt, or NW ≤6 kt | 1st good; 2nd/3rd fun, not linking |
| **Mid / Fun** | 160–175° or 240–270° | 10–13 s | 1.5–3 or 8–10 ft | 0–1 or 4.5–5.5 ft | W–SW onshore 6–10 kt | 1st only (longboard) |
| **Don't Go / Poor** | <155° or >285° | <10 s windswell | <1.5 or >10 ft | <0 or >5.5 ft | Onshore ≥12 kt; within 72 h of rain | — |

- **1st Pt:** soft, consistent wall; works all tides, gets hollower as tide drops; starts ~1.5–2 ft. Since the lagoon restoration, sand settles mid-point and adds a closeout section.
- **2nd Pt:** short, hollow; mid tide; unmakeable on low tide; needs ~3 ft+.
- **3rd Pt:** fastest/steepest outside; mid tide; needs ~3.5–4 ft+.
- Points connect on ~6–8 ft+ at 15 s+.
- W swell wraps through the islands and around Pt Dume, bigger the more W/WSW it is. NW is mostly blocked.
- Season May–Oct, fall best (Santa Anas). Ultra crowded. Chronic water-quality issues near the lagoon outlet.
- Sources disagree on tide direction (falling: Wannasurf; rising: Surfnerd for 3rd).

Sources: wannasurf.com (Malibu) · mondo.surf · surfnerd.com (Malibu Third Point) · socalforecast.blogspot.com (Malibu break map, 2008) · sites.google.com/view/malibu-surfing · theinertia.com (lagoon changes) · thesurfatlas.com · LA County water-quality bulletins · Surfline spot guide: "S, SW, WSW… waist-high to double overhead… medium/mid tide; NW side-offshore".

## El Porto, Manhattan Beach

Faces ~250° (WSW). Offshore = E/ENE. Sandbar beach break and the South Bay's swell magnet, usually 1–2 ft bigger than its neighbours. Sources disagree on which submarine canyon causes this.

| Tier | Swell direction | Period | Face height | Tide | Wind |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Perfect** | W–WNW 265–295° + SSW–SW 200–225° combo | 13–17 s | 5–8 ft | 1.5–3.5 ft, rising | E/ENE ≤10 kt or glassy |
| **Good** | WSW–NW 245–305°, or SW 215–240° | 11–16 s | 4–6 ft | 1–4.5 ft | Offshore <12 kt, or any <6 kt |
| **Mid / Fun** | 200–310° | 9–12 s | 3–4 ft (or 6–10 ft walling) | 0–5.5 ft | Onshore/cross 4–5 kt (≤6 mph) |
| **Don't Go / Poor** | <195° (Palos Verdes), >315° (islands), pure wind swell | <8 s | <2.5 or >10 ft | <0 or >5.5 ft | **Onshore over 6 mph (5.2 kt)** — your call, 2026-11-11; or offshore >20 kt |

- A swell from one direction walls up ("Great Wall of El Porto"); combos create the "holes".
- "Even a light onshore ruins the surf" (surf-forecast.com reviews). The sea breeze typically arrives late morning *(est.)*.
- Low tide: shallow, punchy, rippy. 5 ft+ highs: backwash, fat shorebreak.
- Season Oct–Apr. Water quality is poor after rain (storm drains).
- Sources disagree on the best direction (NW/WNW per surf-forecast & surfnerd vs SW–WNW combos per Surfline) and on tide direction.

Sources: surfnerd.com (El Porto) · surf-forecast.com/breaks/El-Porto-Beach (+ reviews) · en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Porto_Beach · easyreadernews.com/surf-tips-south-bay · surf-forecasting Google Group (2010) · Thomson et al. 2007 (Redondo Canyon) · thesurfatlas.com · Surfline spot guide: "SW, WSW, W, WNW combo… E, SE wind… chest-high to double overhead… mid tide; NW swell magnet".
